The Tiger

The tiger is less tolerant of heat than other large cats, which may explain why it is an adept swimmer that appears to enjoy bathing. The average life span of a tiger in the wild is about 11 years. Tiger cubs are born blind and do not have clear vision for six to eight weeks.

The Jaguar

Jaguars are the largest cats in the Americas and the third-largest big cats globally, after tigers and lions. Jaguars were revered in Mesoamerican civilizations and often associated with gods, warriors, and spiritual power. Adult jaguars live alone and maintain large territories.
